Scope and evidence boundary
---------------------------
This package adds missing publication plumbing, not current-run telemetry.
No scheduler, migration, credentials, mount, deployment or publisher is
activated by this change. Deploying this code alone WILL NOT populate the
current btc-eur-paired-diag-20261110-001 run. Its frozen image
ae924141854362257738322ac9a1022cd47e0b02 has no publisher. Separate, approved
read-only volume access or a coherent operator-controlled transfer has NOT
been established and is a prerequisite, not a result of this work.

Do not edit/restart the running container, change its volumes/host configuration,
read credentials from it, or modify its strategy/source/journal/account state.
Do not alias the legacy host8h run or use immutable completed-report storage.
This lane does not assess profitability, actual exchange fees, process liveness,
operational readiness or automatic promotion. It cannot authorize trading.

OBSERVED_CHECKPOINT means a stable committed journal snapshot was observed.
writer.lock is never read, removed or treated as liveness. A terminal event is
reported as TERMINAL_RECORDED or INTERRUPTED_RECORDED, NOT VERIFIED_COMPLETE.
It is not the terminal evaluator's independent verification. Pending, incoherent,
unrecognized or invalid evidence yields BUSY/UNKNOWN with no publishable payload.

Contract and resource bounds
----------------------------
SPOT_PAPER_PAIRED_PROGRESS_V1 is separate from all final-report schemas. It binds
the full expected run, source/config/research hashes, feed, comparison engine,
model-pair, roles/models/account identities, journal sequence and SHA256 of
JSON.stringify(payload) in the existing journal envelope. Hash verification is
integrity checking, not a signature or independent verification of source code.
The exact reviewed expected identity must be supplied independently of the
journal. Never derive the expected identity from an untrusted upload.

Only the EUR next-run three-role model pair is supported. It is not a USD lane.
Amounts are EUR CENTS. netPnlCents = committed netEquityCents - starting capital.
Never subtract booked fees or modeled slippage again from net equity/PnL.
Slippage is an execution-price assumption; fees are the unverified modeled
20 bps per side EEA taker assumption, slippage 10 bps per side. These are NOT
actual charged exchange fees or evidence of the account's fee tier.

Valuations come only from the last committed shared_observation, not a strategy
replay, a fresh market feed, a later failure, or a terminal report's revaluation.
observedAt/valuationAt survive export/upload unchanged. exportedAt is NOT a
freshness clock. latestEventAt and terminal.at may be later without freshening
financial metrics. Null cash/hypothetical exit costs mean UNKNOWN, not zero;
the journal summary does not separately establish these amounts.

Fills are validated for role, order, quantity/notional rounding, modeled fees,
timestamps and cumulative fee/turnover consistency. At most 12 recent fills
per role are published. fillCount counts executions; completedRoundTripCount
counts alternating completed BUY/SELL pairs, not individual fills. Risk stop
codes are allowlisted; strategy/risk state, paths, raw responses, free-text
errors and secrets are never exported.

Exporter: Linux /proc/self/fd is required. Each directory component is opened
O_NOFOLLOW|O_NONBLOCK|O_DIRECTORY, pinning the directory chain. Journal opens
use O_NOFOLLOW|O_NONBLOCK and regular-file checks, 16 MiB/1024-event/480-observation
caps and a 3 second deadline (configurable library cap 5 seconds). Two bounded
reads and file/directory identity/size/mtime/ctime checks bracket pending-state
checks. A concurrent append, rename or transient pending rewrite is rejected.
No lock, pending marker, source response, ledger or account file is modified.
Missing required committed valuations are UNKNOWN; there is no legacy fallback.

Transport: 64 KiB progress cap, 5 second request-body cap, two concurrent gateway
requests and two bounded store operations. Pool statement/lock/connection
deadlines are reused from the reviewed private report pool. Publisher: one
8 second request by default (maximum 10 seconds), 4 KiB receipt cap, fixed HTTPS
endpoint, no redirects and no retry. A timeout/lost response can occur after
a database commit: UNKNOWN requires operator inspection, not blind retry.
Browser connector: default disabled, at most one read, maximum 10 second deadline.
Capacity stays busy until uncooperative underlying work actually settles.
