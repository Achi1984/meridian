# Portfolio history price provenance

Canonical history requires every non-Pionex spot holding to have a finite positive mark, a source, and a per-symbol fetch time no later than capture and no older than 15 minutes. A supplied provider observation time is checked separately; when the provider supplies none, it remains `null`. Local `fetchedAt`/`ts` is receipt time, never provider observation time. The global `livePriceMeta.fresh` flag cannot qualify a symbol.

The history runtime records fetched marks in `livePriceMeta.perSymbol` and `livePrices[symbol].ts`; failed or partial fetches update attempt diagnostics only and leave retained marks and their provenance unchanged. Existing R18 `BINANCE_SPOT` marks with per-symbol `ts` remain supported. The current app quote producer now labels its local receipt time as `fetchedAt` and explicitly leaves provider observation time unknown; `updatedAt` alone is not admission provenance. Other legacy marks, including price-only values, remain usable by existing valuation/rendering paths but have unknown history freshness until a supported producer supplies explicit fetch provenance.

History stores the bounded per-symbol marks, source, fetch/observation times, capture time, source quality, and fallback reason in the existing `source_status` JSON. No schema migration or historical rewrite is needed. Old rows without `spotPriceProvenance` are excluded from canonical history as unknown legacy provenance; they are not modified or declared corrupted. Incomplete new captures are returned with diagnostics but are never inserted as complete points.
