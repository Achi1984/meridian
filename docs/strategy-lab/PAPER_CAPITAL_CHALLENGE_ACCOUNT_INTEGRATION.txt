IDENTITY AND MODELED COST CONTRACT

The immutable binding has exactly campaignId, currency, strategyVersion,
costVersion. campaignId must equal the existing policy campaign, currency
must be USD, and strategyVersion must be a nonempty bounded safe identifier.
Every event repeats that binding. A journal cannot switch campaign, currency,
strategy or cost version partway through. A strategy migration requires a
separately reviewed continuation design preserving the complete account and
guard history; creating a new journal must not be used to reset a campaign.

USD_MODELED_COSTS.version = BTC_USD_TAKER20_ADVERSE10_V1:
  20bps taker fee EACH side
  10bps adverse slippage EACH side
  ASSUMED_COUNTERFACTUAL; venue/account applicability NOT_VERIFIED

These are assumptions, not a verified USD venue fee schedule or actual
liquidity. The provisional maximum single entry remains USD100.00 gross
modeled notional (10000 cents); its entry fee is additional, so maximum cash
debit is 10020 cents. This is not an optimized sizing recommendation.
The account starts from native USD100000 cents and zero BTC. It is long-only,
one open inventory lot at a time; subsequent buys while holding are skipped.
Partial sells are supported. No leverage, borrowing, top-ups, resets, maker
fills, forced churn or pause override exists. Even a paused exit must have a
valid fresh native-USD quote, positive quantity no greater than held BTC,
and enough cash to cover any cent-rounded fee exceeding tiny sale proceeds.

EXACT ARITHMETIC AND EVERY COST ONCE

All money outputs are safe integer USD cents, never EUR or stablecoin units.
BTC quantities and USD/BTC prices enter as plain decimal STRINGS with at
most 8 fractional digits, no exponent/sign/NaN/coercion; their scaled integers
must be <= Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER. BTC is stored as decimal integer-string
satoshis. Prices use 1e8 USD units. BigInt intermediates preserve the exact
adverse price as a rational; no intermediate price rounding or floats.

Buy price = ask * 10010/10000.
Sell price = bid * 9990/10000.
Gross USD cents = quantitySatoshis * priceScaled * adverseMultiplier
                  / (100000000000000 * 10000).
Buy debit notional rounds UP to cents; sell proceeds/marks round DOWN.
Each fee rounds UP to the next whole cent from 20bps of the EXACT adverse
notional, not from its already-rounded cent value. Zero inventory has zero
exit fee; a positive sub-cent trade can still have a one-cent fee.

Entry cash subtracts buy notional AND entry fee. Exit cash adds sell notional
AND subtracts exit fee. At every event:
  netEquityCents = cashCents
                + inventoryAtAdverseBidCents
                - unbookedAccruedFeesCents
                - modeledExitCostsCents.
Inventory is marked at bid minus slippage. modeledExitCostsCents contains
only the as-yet-unbooked modeled taker liquidation fee on remaining inventory.
Spread is already captured by ask/bid, slippage by the adverse price. Neither
is subtracted again. Booked entry/sale fees are not accrued fees. The report's
modeledSlippageCents is a rounded diagnostic, NEVER a second cash deduction.
After a partial sale, only the remaining BTC has a liquidation fee reserve.
After a complete sale, the reserve is zero.

ACCRUE_FEE explicitly records an ADDITIONAL hypothetical USD fee, not a
repeat of modeled trade fees. It increases only unbookedAccruedFeesCents.
SETTLE_FEES subtracts that liability from cash and zeros it, preserving
equity on an unchanged quote. Accruals may expose negative net equity;
losses and drawdown overshoot are never clamped or hidden. Settlement cannot
make cash negative. These caller assertions are not verified venue invoices.

QUOTE EVIDENCE AND BRIDGE

Every event requires a quote with EXACT keys:
  schemaVersion: NATIVE_BTC_USD_QUOTE_V1
  id: bounded safe identifier
  marketPair: BTC/USD
  baseCurrency: BTC
  quoteCurrency: USD
  sourceId: bounded safe identifier
  nativeInstrument: BTC/USD (normalized native instrument assertion)
  evidenceKind: SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE or OFFLINE_CALLER_ASSERTION
  bid, ask: positive decimal strings
  quotedAtMs, receivedAtMs: nonnegative safe integer epoch milliseconds
  maxAgeMs: positive integer <= policy.maxValuationAgeMs (10000)

bid <= ask, quotedAtMs <= receivedAtMs <= event.observedAtMs, and quote age
<= maxAgeMs are required. EUR/USDT/USDC, future/stale/malformed/crossed/
nonpositive evidence is rejected, including for exits. Across unique events
both quote timestamps are nondecreasing. Within a source, reusing a quote ID
with different evidence is contradictory. Equal quote timestamps are allowed
within freshness; new account event/guard observation timestamps are strict.

Offline caller evidence IS NOT authenticated live acquisition. Relabeling an
EUR feed as this schema cannot prove native USD applicability. A future
trusted acquisition adapter must bind the actual venue-native instrument,
source payload and acquisition timestamps independently; it cannot claim
authentication just by supplying this record. This implementation deliberately
does not accept an AUTHENTICATED_LIVE label.

createUsdObservation({binding, account, quote, id, observedAtMs}) is a pure
bridge returning EXACTLY the observation schema accepted by existing guard,
without adding cost/evidence fields to that strict schema. A standalone caller
balance is hypothetical, not authoritative state. Authoritative hypothetical
account derivation uses replayUsdAccount, never a supplied account snapshot.
Full quote/cost/fill bindings remain in the associated account transactions.

APPEND-ONLY EVENT INPUT AND REPLAY

createUsdAccountJournal(binding) creates the empty pure input contract:
  {schemaVersion: USD_PAPER_ACCOUNT_EVENTS_V1, binding, events: []}

Each event has EXACT keys:
  id, campaignId, currency, strategyVersion, costVersion, observedAtMs,
  action, quote, quantityBtc, feeCents, reason

action is BUY, SELL, HOLD, SKIP_ENTRY, ACCRUE_FEE or SETTLE_FEES.
BUY/SELL quantityBtc is a positive decimal string; other actions use null.
Only ACCRUE_FEE has positive safe integer feeCents; all others use zero.
Only SKIP_ENTRY has a bounded safe identifier reason; all others use null.
HOLD is a valuation observation, not a fill. SKIP_ENTRY records a strategy
decision supplied by the caller; it does not invent a trading signal.

appendUsdAccountEvent(journal, event) validates complete prior evidence and
returns a new frozen journal. Existing inputs remain unchanged/unfrozen.
Exact repeated event IDs are idempotent (even old retries); contradictory
duplicates are rejected, including quantity, quote, action and binding.
replayUsdAccount(journal) derives balances, modeled fills, observations,
guard state and activity deterministically from the ordered evidence.
No caller-supplied state, balance snapshot, peak or pause field is admitted.
All supplied raw journal lengths are bounded to 256, including retry entries;
append normalizes exact retries without consuming additional observation slots.
It does not truncate observations or roll history forward beyond that limit.

A hypothetical BUY is skipped for DRAWDOWN_PAUSED, OPEN_INVENTORY,
MAX_ENTRY_EXCEEDED, or INSUFFICIENT_UNENCUMBERED_CASH. These reasons are
reported. Invalid evidence throws rather than silently replacing a price.
Every accepted unique event produces one post-accounting observation through
advanceChallengeState. New buys are flat-only; prior booked/accrued flat
losses have already been observed. Holds retain open losses; sells are only
risk-reducing. A fee/entry loss can latch the pause on its own event; pause
does not undo that recorded hypothetical event or automatically flatten it.

The returned authorization always has executionAuthorized=false and
liveAcquisitionAuthenticated=false. productionExposureGate is the REAL
getExposureGate result: POLICY_DISABLED while unpaused, DRAWDOWN_PAUSED
when paused. Hypothetical fills do not bypass that gate for authorization.
Nothing in these modules dispatches an order or admits a production bot.

PERSISTENCE HANDOFF TO #1297 (NO HISTORY-STORE FILE CHANGES)

createUsdPersistencePayload(journal) returns USD_PAPER_ACCOUNT_PERSISTENCE_V1:
  binding, costs, transactions: [{event, fill, skipReason, observation}],
  guardObservations, account, guardState,
  accountTransactionDurability: NOT_IMPLEMENTED,
  executionAuthorized: false.

Modeled fills include event/quote/source IDs, time, complete binding, exact
price rational, side, quantitySatoshis, notionalCents, feeCents, slippageCents,
ASSUMED_COUNTERFACTUAL provenance and MODELED_TAKER liquidity. Observations
bind via event.id; no independently supplied fill/valuation claim is trusted.
Derived payloads are diagnostic outputs, not restart admission credentials.
To verify one, replay its original events and compare all derived output.

#1297 is integrated from main, and its postchecks are complete. The available
guard-history interface for observation-only history is:
  for each NEW derived observation in order:
    result = await historyStore.append(observation)
    independently retain result.tip
  resume only via reopenExisting(directory, {expectedTip: externallyRetainedTip})
  compare persisted observations to replayed account observations before use.

The observation-only history store is not used by the USD account journal.
It cannot provide full account-transaction durability by itself: saving
observations alone loses BTC/fill/cost evidence. The in-memory append API
remains a pure replay helper and is not itself a durable log.

VERSIONED FULL-ACCOUNT JOURNAL V2 AND OFFLINE RUNNER

scripts/paper-capital-challenge/account-store.mjs composes the existing
account replay/valuation/guard with the existing CheckpointStore transaction
primitive. Its sole durable checkpoint contains the immutable campaign,
strategy, cost and native BTC/USD binding plus each complete admitted
transaction (input event/quote/action, derived modeled fill or skip reason,
and guard valuation observation). Sequence and SHA-256 predecessor digest
cover every record. Cash, BTC, booked and accrued costs, high-water equity
and latched pause are reconstructed by replaying the complete event list;
serialized derived values are checked against replay and are never trusted
as restart snapshots. There is no separate guard journal.

The versioned contract is USD_PAPER_ACCOUNT_JOURNAL_V2 /
USD_PAPER_ACCOUNT_TIP_V2. createNew is create-only; reopenExisting requires
the exact independently retained expectedTip and matching binding. The
checkpoint lock enforces one writer. Exact duplicate IDs return the existing
tip; conflicting IDs fail. Any unacknowledged commit blocks that writer.
A crash with a pending marker remains UNKNOWN/locked, whether the checkpoint
rename happened or not. Inspection does not clear a marker or stale lock.
Never retry a trade after an ambiguous commit or reopen without the external
tip. There is no reset, top-up, compaction, or truncation path.

Predeclared offline resource bound: at most 256 complete events, at most
24 hours from the first admitted event, and at most 512 KiB for the complete
canonical checkpoint. The 256-event bound is inherited from the unchanged
V1 account/guard replay contract; V2 does not silently increase or alter its
replay semantics. The full-window test writes and reopens all 256 events,
asserts the byte and cumulative-publication limits, and verifies the event
bound. When the 256th event or 24-hour horizon is reached, the next unique
event is rejected and the process must stop. This is not a guarantee that a
24-hour feed at a higher cadence fits: no safe continuation/retention beyond
these exact limits is implemented or claimed.

The measured full-window fixture spans 86,062,500 ms. Its final canonical
checkpoint is 331,375 bytes (512 KiB allowed), cumulative published checkpoint
bytes are 42,648,244 (64 MiB allowed), and 256 append/replay/fsync operations
took 5,870 ms in the focused local run. These figures are bounded synthetic
test evidence, not a host capacity or latency guarantee.

scripts/paper-capital-challenge/offline-runner.mjs consumes prevalidated
caller-supplied native USD quotes and one injected causal decision per quote.
It persists each action through the account journal and reports activity,
rejected entries, last modeled trade, booked/additional costs, net equity,
and remaining drawdown allowance. The real guard blocks buys after pause;
valid risk-reducing sells remain admissible. It has no provider, host,
network, order dispatch, or live authentication. A future host still needs
an independently qualified native BTC/USD feed and an independently retained
latest tip, supplied back to reopenExisting after restart.

ACTIVITY AND LAUNCH BLOCKERS

Activity reports completed flat-to-open-to-flat round trips, exposure duration
through the last observation (including an unclosed position), open start time,
skipped-entry reasons, booked modeled fees, additional accrued fees, modeled
slippage diagnostic, gross turnover, current reserved exit fees, maximum
high-water drawdown, net equity and net PnL INCLUDING open losses/exit reserve.
activityCriterionMet requires at least one completed round trip. It is only a
minimal activity criterion, not strategy approval or profitability evidence.
profitableCandidateEstablished is always false. Cash-only and held-but-never-
closed candidates do not pass that criterion. No forced trades, churn to pass
it, or pause override should be introduced by a future strategy integration.

Launch blockers remain: independently retained external restart tip;
reviewed continuation beyond the 256-event/24-hour/512-KiB bounds;
authenticated native live BTC/USD feed/venue applicability; verified USD
account cost schedule; host wiring and explicit owner authorization/admission;
an approved active strategy and sufficient counterfactual activity evidence
without bypassing drawdown. All production policy gates remain disabled.
