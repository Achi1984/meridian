## Proposal as recorded—not selected by this dossier

The metadata describes seven proposed Kraken USD daily series, in order: **XBTUSD, ETHUSD, SOLUSD, XRPUSD, LINKUSD, AVAXUSD, SUIUSD**. The D1 draft records 955 synchronized daily rows per series, 2024-02-19 through 2026-09-30, and the normalized-file and selected-series digests. These are recorded identities, not a new inspection or authentication of captured files; the production input remains `NOT_FROZEN`. ([request, assets and row range](../../research/protocols/b1-7-20261018/EXECUTION_REQUEST.md#L121-L142); [candidate input state](../research/d2-candidate/admission-candidate.json#L16-L24))

The proposed scope is **one offline trial and exactly 44 ordered paths**: 5 variants × 3 scenarios × 2 segments (30); full basket × 3 × 2 (6); 2 frozen controls × 3 scenarios × diagnostic segment (6); and cash × 2 segments (2). Limits are 30 minutes per batch, 60 seconds per path, and 1 MiB serialized output. No retries or adaptive trials; a failure stops the batch, leaves remaining paths unstarted, makes prior paths incomplete, and produces no partial ranking. Engine checks are cooperative, so a separate supervised runner would have to enforce a hard wall-clock limit. A proposed one-trial scope is not evidence that prior attempts are unused or that a remaining budget exists. ([execution request](../../research/protocols/b1-7-20261018/EXECUTION_REQUEST.md#L143-L150); [trial-ledger limits](HISTORICAL_TRIAL_LEDGER_20261015.md#L17-L18))

**Costs are assumptions:** 15 bp per side baseline (10 bp spot taker reference + 5 bp assumed friction), 30 bp per side stress, and 30 bp plus a one-day delayed fill. The inputs are pinned Kraken spot prices with OKX-inspired assumed costs—not OKX execution evidence, measured spread/impact, account-specific fees, historical fee reconstruction, or maker/futures/funding evidence. ([request, cost assumptions](../../research/protocols/b1-7-20261018/EXECUTION_REQUEST.md#L147-L148))

**The recorded screen** requires positive diagnostic primary net return; sampled daily-close drawdown ≤25%; return no worse than either frozen control and drawdown no worse than either; at least 20% relative drawdown reduction versus the full basket; positive primary at 30 bp both without delay and with one-day delay; and at least 3/4 neighbors positive at 15 bp and separately at 30 bp without delay. Delayed neighbors are report-only. Invalid/zero risk denominators yield `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; comparisons are diagnostic-only and a pass is historical-candidate evidence, not a holdout or significance claim. The screen is `NOT_RUN`. ([request, screen](../../research/protocols/b1-7-20261018/EXECUTION_REQUEST.md#L148-L150); [candidate state](../research/d2-candidate/admission-candidate.json#L16-L24))

The requested exception is **research-stage portfolio-level no-stop analysis only**: no finite per-trade stop is specified or invented; CRV is `UNDEFINED_NO_FINITE_STOP`, null, and not applicable; no trade CRV or promotion eligibility is claimed. The 15 bp baseline control mean uses the primary path's held target over all 180 development days; initial fill is day one, and delayed paths apply the zero-before-fill rule. ([request, exception](../../research/protocols/b1-7-20261018/EXECUTION_REQUEST.md#L152-L159))
